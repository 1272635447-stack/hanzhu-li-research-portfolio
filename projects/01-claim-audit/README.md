# Project 01 — AI-Generated Research-Claim Audit

## Overview

This work began with an audit of **40 claims across five AI-generated research cards**, checking each claim against its cited research paper. The review recorded issues including method-description drift, terminology shifts, omitted findings or qualifications, and claims that extended beyond what the cited evidence established.

The audit motivated a claim-level approach: rather than asking only whether a cited paper is relevant, inspect which parts of the source support, qualify, contradict, or fail to establish each component of a particular claim.

## Work completed

- Reviewed 40 AI-generated claims against cited source papers.
- Recorded discrepancies involving methods, terminology, omitted qualifications, and evidence-to-claim overreach.
- Developed a bounded 12-item claim–evidence stimulus set for subsequent interface work.
- Used a five-label scheme: Supported, Supported with caveat, Contradicted, Not Established, and Insufficient evidence.

## Interpretation and limitations

This is a researcher-led audit and materials-development exercise. The records should not be represented as independent expert consensus or a contemporaneous independent blind review. Any retrospective AI-assisted reconstruction is identified as such.
